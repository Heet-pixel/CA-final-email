CA Akash Patel

Partner | Taxation, Dispute Resolution & Forensic Audit Expert (B.Com, ISA – ICAI, ACA)

CA Akash Patel is a Chartered Accountant specialising in Income Tax Litigation, Search & Seizure Assessments, Tax Investigations and Complex Assessment Proceedings, with significant expertise in Real Estate Taxation, Land Transactions and Real Estate Advisory. He possesses strong professional capabilities in tax strategy, dispute resolution, financial structuring and regulatory compliance. His experience also encompasses financial reporting, finalisation of books of accounts and statutory compliance across manufacturing, real estate, manpower services, food chains and professional services.

Core strengths & capabilities

  • Income tax and GST scrutiny assessments, appeals and representation before authorities
  • Direct and indirect tax: TDS, VAT, Service Tax, Excise and GST
  • Internal, government, bank and insurance audits
  • Real estate accounting and regulatory compliance (including RERA)
  • Forensic audit support in Income Tax Department search & seizure matters
  • Data driven evidence analysis for complex investigations
  • Defensible tax positions that minimise fiscal exposure
  • Methodical case preparation and sustainable remediation
