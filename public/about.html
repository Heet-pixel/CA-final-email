Who We Are

At AVKAS & Co, our team of experts combines deep technical knowledge with creative thinking. Founded in 2021, we launched with a vision to translate technical expertise into strategic advantage: “turning regulatory complexity, financial data and legal risk into clear decisions, measurable outcomes and long-term value” for our clients.

In just a few years, we have built an impressive record of growth: we have served 500+ clients and grown to over 30 team members. Today, we partner with entrepreneurs, family businesses, corporates and investors in India and abroad, supporting clients across various industries. Our multidisciplinary team blends chartered accountancy rigour, legal insight and modern technology to deliver practical, commercially-minded solutions that protect value and accelerate growth.

Our founding story and rapid growth reflect the strong brand identity of AVKAS & Co. From day one, we have focused on practical collaboration and creative problem-solving. This clear narrative born in 2021 from a commitment to turn expertise into business advantage guides everything we do.

How We Work

Our approach is built around partnership: we don’t hand over a report and leave. Instead, we become an embedded extension of your team, moving from diagnosis to delivery and continuous improvement.

  1. 01

    Listen & diagnose

    We start by listening: fast, focused discovery that surfaces the real problems (not just the obvious ones). We review controls, reporting, tax positions and commercial levers to prioritise the issues that will create the most value quickly.

  2. 02

    Design & enable

    Solutions are customised, not canned. Whether it’s a governance framework, a cashflow and forecasting model, an IP/tax structure, or a compliance program, we design processes and tools that fit your operating reality: simple to use, hard to break.

  3. 03

    Implement & measure

    Advice without implementation is wasted effort. We support rollout, train your team and embed dashboards and KPIs so progress is visible. Every engagement has measurable targets such as fewer exceptions, faster closes, cash preserved, margins improved, or disputes resolved.

  4. 04

    Embed & evolve

    Business conditions change; good solutions evolve. We stay on as trusted advisors, refining controls and forecasts, adapting legal structures and strengthening governance so your organisation becomes more resilient and future ready.

Throughout this cycle we prioritise responsiveness, clarity and commercial judgment. We convert technical outputs into decision tools for founders, finance teams, boards and investors, delivering work that’s usable, explainable and aligned with your strategic priorities.

Why our clients stay

You may be satisfied with the results, but we aren’t, and that makes all the difference. We continuously strive to improve, iterate and raise the bar, delivering work that is more accurate, more insightful and more durable. Rather than stopping at a completed deliverable, we measure outcomes, refine solutions and proactively hunt for further value so our work becomes simpler to use, harder to break and easier to scale. In short: we never settle for “good enough.”
