Backed by a comprehensive academic and professional foundation including a Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com),Master of commerce(M.com) ,a Bachelor of Laws (LLB) providing rigorous legal training, specialized DISA accreditation, and credentialed expertise as a Social Auditor (ISAI) he seamlessly integrates traditional accounting standards with progressive legal perspectives, IT audit controls, and modern social accountability mandates.
